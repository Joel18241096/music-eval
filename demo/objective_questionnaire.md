# 六大主观指标：

Overall Musical Quality：整体质量（最终用户感知）
Vocal & Accompaniment Quality：生成质量（基础能力）
Vocal-Accompaniment Harmony：双轨协同（LeVo/Muse 都关注）
Song Structure Clarity：结构规划（SongSculpt核心）
Lyric Accuracy：歌词演唱能力（Song generation必须）
Instruction Faithfulness：可控性（SongSculpt核心）

# Subjective Evaluation Questionnaire

For each generated song, please rate the following aspects on a **1–5 scale**, where **5 indicates the best quality**.

---

## 1. Overall Musical Quality

**Question**

> How would you rate the overall musical quality of this generated song?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5 (Excellent)** | Sounds like a professionally produced complete song with excellent musicality and overall listening experience. |
| **4 (Good)** | Generally natural and enjoyable, with only minor imperfections. |
| **3 (Fair)** | Acceptable overall, although several aspects noticeably reduce the listening experience. |
| **2 (Poor)** | Significant quality issues that frequently interrupt musical coherence. |
| **1 (Bad)** | Overall musical quality is very poor and difficult to listen to. |

---

## 2. Vocal & Accompaniment Quality

**Question**

> How would you rate the quality of both the singing voice and instrumental accompaniment?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5** | Vocals are natural and expressive, accompaniment is rich and realistic, and both exhibit consistently high audio quality. |
| **4** | Mostly natural with only occasional artifacts or unnatural sounds. |
| **3** | Moderate quality with noticeable imperfections but still generally acceptable. |
| **2** | Frequent artifacts or unnatural singing/instrumentation reduce listening quality. |
| **1** | Severe artifacts substantially degrade the overall audio quality. |

---

## 3. Vocal–Accompaniment Harmony

**Question**

> How well do the vocals and accompaniment work together as a complete song?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5** | Vocals and accompaniment are highly synchronized, balanced, and mutually supportive throughout the song. |
| **4** | Generally well coordinated with only minor mismatches. |
| **3** | Coordination is acceptable but inconsistent across different sections. |
| **2** | Frequent imbalance or mismatch between vocals and accompaniment. |
| **1** | Vocals and accompaniment sound disconnected or incompatible. |

---

## 4. Song Structure Clarity

**Question**

> How clear, coherent, and well-organized is the overall song structure?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5** | Song structure is very clear, with well-organized sections that form a coherent musical progression. |
| **4** | Overall structure is clear with only minor organizational inconsistencies. |
| **3** | Structure is generally recognizable but lacks consistency in some sections. |
| **2** | Structure is difficult to follow due to weak or inconsistent organization. |
| **1** | No clear or coherent song structure can be identified. |

---

## 5. Lyric Accuracy

**Question**

> How accurately are the provided lyrics sung in the generated song?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5** | Lyrics are sung accurately and clearly with almost no omissions, substitutions, or pronunciation errors. |
| **4** | Minor lyric errors that do not affect overall understanding. |
| **3** | Several noticeable lyric mistakes but most lyrics remain recognizable. |
| **2** | Frequent lyric errors substantially affect intelligibility. |
| **1** | Lyrics are largely incorrect, unintelligible, or missing. |

---

## 6. Instruction Faithfulness

**Question**

> How well does the generated song follow the given textual instruction?

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **5** | Fully reflects the requested style, emotion, structure, instrumentation, and other specified conditions. |
| **4** | Most requested conditions are satisfied with only minor deviations. |
| **3** | Roughly follows the instruction but misses several important conditions. |
| **2** | Only a small portion of the instruction is reflected. |
| **1** | The generated song is largely unrelated to the given instruction. |
